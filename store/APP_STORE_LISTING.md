## Description (max 4000)
You broke a bone. The truth is boring: you tripped on a curb, you slipped in
the kitchen, you "moved a couch." Nobody wants that version.

How'd You Do That? takes the dull facts and hands you back a story worth
telling — the one you'll actually repeat at the party.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us what you broke (pick as many bones as you managed).
2. Add the boring facts: where it happened and what you were really doing.
3. Pick a vibe.
4. Get a short, spoken-sounding story plus a few one-liners you can text.

ELEVEN VIBES
Absurd, Heroic, Dramatic, Mysterious, Wholesome, Conspiracy, Fairytale,
Sportscast, Corporate, True Crime, and Western. Each one changes how the
story is told — from breathless play-by-play to grave true-crime narration.

DIAL THE SPICE
A simple slider takes you from squeaky-clean to cheeky (always kept tasteful).

MADE TO SHARE
Every story comes with a designed image card, ready for the group chat or your
story. Don't like the first take? Reroll it, make it wilder, or flip back to
the version you liked. Save the keepers to your personal library.

FEELING LAZY?
Hit Surprise Me and we'll roll everything for you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
On supported devices, stories can be written by Apple's on-device intelligence
— free, fast, and offline. You choose whether the app may use cloud AI as a
fallback; keep it off and story details never leave your device. Saved stories
live only on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Now go tell it right.

## What's New (version 1.0)
First release. Eleven vibes, a spice slider, shareable story cards, a personal
library, and Surprise Me. Go break a leg (responsibly).

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers
- **Do you collect data?** Yes (only when the cloud generation tier is used).
- **Data type:** User Content → "Other User Content" (the free-text location
  and activity you type).
  - Linked to identity: **No**
  - Used for tracking: **No**
  - Purpose: **App Functionality**
- No other data types (no contact info, identifiers, usage analytics,
  diagnostics, location services, etc.).
- Rationale: when the on-device tier is unavailable, your typed inputs are sent
  to our proxy and on to a third-party model to generate the story. They aren't
  stored by us or tied to your identity. Saved stories stay on-device.

## Review notes (paste into "Notes for the reviewer")
- No login required; all features are reachable immediately.
- Stories are generated by an AI model. Content is moderated: user input is
  screened before generation and model output is screened before display, with
  a fallback to a fixed on-device template generator. A "Report this story"
  action is available on every result (ellipsis menu, top-right).
- Before the first generation, the app explains cloud fallback and lets the
  user allow it or keep all story details on-device. The choice remains
  available in the final setup step.
- The higher "spice" setting produces cheeky but PG-13 humor.
