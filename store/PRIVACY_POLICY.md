# Privacy Policy — How'd You Do That?

_Last updated: 2026-08-28_

How'd You Do That? ("the app") is designed to collect as little as possible.
This policy explains what happens to what you type.

## What you type
To generate a story, you enter a few details — such as where an injury happened
and what you were doing. This text is used only to create your story.

## When text leaves your device
The app writes stories in one of two ways:

- **On-device (preferred).** On supported devices, stories are generated
  entirely on your phone using Apple's on-device intelligence. In this mode,
  the text you type **never leaves your device**.
- **Cloud fallback.** If on-device generation isn't available, the text you
  type is sent over an encrypted connection to our story service (hosted on
  Cloudflare) and then to a third-party AI provider (such as Groq or Google
  Gemini) solely to generate your story and return it.

In the cloud case, your text is processed to produce the story and is **not**
stored by us, is **not** linked to your identity, and is **not** used to track
you or for advertising. Third-party providers process the request under their
own terms; we send only the story details you enter, nothing that identifies
you.

## What stays on your device
Stories you choose to **Save** are stored **only on your device**. We have no
account system and no server-side copy of your library. Deleting a saved story,
or deleting the app, removes it.

## What we don't collect
No account or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No
contacts, photos, precise location, or device identifiers. We don't build a
profile of you.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Reporting content
Every generated story has a "Report this story" option. Reports come to us by
email so we can review and improve the app's safeguards.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version at
