# Privacy Policy — How'd You Do That?

_Last updated: 2026-08-28_

How'd You Do That? ("the app") is designed to collect as little as possible.
This policy explains what happens to what you type.

## What you type
To generate a story, you enter a few details — such as where an injury happened
and what you were doing. This text is used only to create your story.

## When text leaves your device
The app writes stories in one of two ways:

- **On-device (preferred).** On supported devices, stories are generated
  entirely on your phone using Apple's on-device intelligence. In this mode,
  the text you type **never leaves your device**.
- **Cloud fallback (when you allow it).** The first time you make a story, the
  app asks whether cloud fallback may be used. If you allow it and on-device
  generation isn't available or takes too long, the text you type is sent over
  an encrypted connection to our story service (hosted on Cloudflare) and then
  to a third-party AI provider (such as Groq or Google Gemini) solely to
  generate your story and return it. You can change this choice from the
  “Allow cloud AI fallback” toggle in the story setup flow.

If you keep generation on your device, your text is never sent to our service.
When Apple's on-device model is unavailable, the app uses its built-in classic
story generator instead.

In the cloud case, your text is processed to produce the story and is **not**
stored by us, is **not** linked to your identity, and is **not** used to track
you or for advertising. We send only the story details you enter. Our providers
are required to protect this data consistently with this policy and applicable
law.

To prevent automated abuse of the cloud service, Cloudflare uses the
connection's network address as a short-lived rate-limiting key. It is not
included in the story prompt, sent to the AI provider, tied to an account, or
used for tracking or advertising.

## What stays on your device
Stories you choose to **Save** are stored **only on your device**. We have no
account system and no server-side copy of your library. Deleting a saved story,
or deleting the app, removes it.

## What we don't collect
No account or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No
contacts, photos, precise location, or device identifiers. We don't build a
profile of you.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Reporting content
Every generated story has a "Report this story" option. Reports come to us by
email so we can review and improve the app's safeguards. A report email includes
the generated story. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to
respond, address safety issues, and meet legal obligations. You may request its
deletion by contacting us below.
