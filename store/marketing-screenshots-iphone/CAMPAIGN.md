# iPhone App Store marketing campaign

Created from the final 1320 × 2868 iPhone simulator captures with the built-in
image generation tool in `ads-marketing` edit mode. The finished panels were
normalized to the original 6.9-inch portrait dimensions.

## Visual direction

- Saturated coral-orange background with a soft cream glow and paper grain
- Sparse cream line doodles tailored to each feature
- One centered black iPhone with a grounded product shadow
- Rounded, oversized white display typography
- Real app UI retained inside the phone

## Campaign copy

1. `START HERE` — `Pick the damage.`
2. `SET THE SCENE` — `Tell the boring truth.`
3. `MAKE IT YOURS` — `Pick your vibe.`
4. `FINE-TUNE IT` — `Turn up the flavor.`
5. `THE PAYOFF` — `Get the better story.`

## Shared generation prompt

Create a premium, playful iOS App Store marketing panel from the supplied app
screenshot. Match the established warm coral campaign, phone scale, rounded
white typography, soft cream radial glow, subtle paper grain, sparse
feature-specific doodles, generous safe margins, and soft device shadow.
Preserve every app UI word, punctuation mark, icon, color, selection, control
state, layout, and status detail exactly and legibly inside one realistic black
iPhone. Do not add people, hands, duplicate devices, extra UI, logos,
watermarks, or unrequested text.
